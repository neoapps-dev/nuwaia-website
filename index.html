About Nuwaia

Science, innovation and green entrepreneurship

Our Vision

To become Iraq's leading green innovation and circular-economy hub, empowering youth and transforming universities into engines of sustainable development.

Our Mission

To enable university students to turn their research ideas into productive, eco-friendly projects that support Iraq's green economy and sustainable growth.

Our Concept

A startup, not just a lab

Nuwaia is not just an environmental lab, but a productive startup that connects science, innovation and the market — turning academic knowledge into real products and green job opportunities.

Why Nuwaia

Tomorrow begins at university

University students transform their majors into practical projects, creating a link between education and the job market through the sustainable economy cycle.

How We Work

From research idea to market

  1. Scientific research starts at the university, grounded in real academic knowledge.
  2. Ideas are transformed into a working prototype.
  3. Experimental production runs through the environmental lab.
  4. We go to market through entrepreneurial partnerships with the private sector.
Our Services

What we do

Supporting startups and educational institutions in adopting green innovation, recycling and sustainability solutions.

Training Workshops

Hands-on training workshops in environmental sustainability and circular economy, targeting students and professionals across sectors.

Design & Manufacturing

Local design and manufacturing of eco-friendly machines and devices, developed by young Iraqi innovators to support recycling and environmental resource management.

Consulting Services

Environmental and technical consulting supporting startups and educational institutions in adopting green innovation and sustainability solutions.

Enhance Recycling Efficiency

Design and implementation of organic waste management systems within institutions and universities to enhance recycling efficiency and reduce environmental impact.

Environmental Awareness

Design of green gardens and sustainable learning spaces within universities and community centers to promote environmental awareness.

Our Products

Made from waste, made to last

Sustainable Vertical Farming Systems

We develop smart vertical farming systems designed for limited spaces, applying modern techniques to reduce water and energy consumption while increasing local food production.

Locally Manufactured Devices

We design and manufacture innovative machines and devices inside the university, built by young Iraqi talents to recycle organic, water and plastic waste — supporting local innovation and sustainable green industry.

Solid Organic Fertilizers

Nuwaia produces two types of environmentally friendly solid fertilizers: Organic Compost and Vermicompost.

Organic Nutrient Blends

Natural elements rich in iron, phosphorus and potassium (NPK), used to improve plant growth and increase soil fertility in environmentally friendly ways.

Liquid Fertilizer (GeoTea)

A natural organic solution rich in nutrients and active microbes, used as a foliar or soil fertilizer to increase plant strength and productivity in a safe way.

Eco-Friendly Plastic Products

We recycle plastic waste to produce useful tools and products for agriculture and community use — contributing to pollution reduction and promoting the circular economy.
